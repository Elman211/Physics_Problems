\## 2. Systems of Equations



\*\*Equations:\*\*

1\.  $2x + 3y = 12$

2\.  $x - y = 1$



\*\*Method: Substitution\*\*



From equation (2), we isolate $x$:

$$x = y + 1$$



Substitute this value of $x$ into equation (1):

$$2(y + 1) + 3y = 12$$



Expand and simplify:

$$2y + 2 + 3y = 12$$

$$5y + 2 = 12$$

$$5y = 10$$

$$\\mathbf{y = 2}$$



Now, find $x$ by substituting $y$ back into the isolated equation:

$$x = 2 + 1$$

$$\\mathbf{x = 3}$$



\*\*Final Solution:\*\* $(3, 2)$
