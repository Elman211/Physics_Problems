\## 4. Rearranging Formulas

\*\*Formula:\*\* The period of a simple pendulum is given by:

$$T = 2\\pi \\sqrt{\\frac{L}{g}}$$



\*\*Goal:\*\* Isolate $g$ (acceleration due to gravity).



\### Derivation Steps:

1\. \*\*Divide by $2\\pi$:\*\*

&#x20;  $$\\frac{T}{2\\pi} = \\sqrt{\\frac{L}{g}}$$

2\. \*\*Square both sides:\*\*

&#x20;  $$\\frac{T^2}{4\\pi^2} = \\frac{L}{g}$$

3\. \*\*Invert both sides:\*\*

&#x20;  $$\\frac{4\\pi^2}{T^2} = \\frac{g}{L}$$

4\. \*\*Multiply by $L$:\*\*

&#x20;  $$\\mathbf{g = \\frac{4\\pi^2 L}{T^2}}$$

\## 5. Trigonometry: Vector Components

\*\*Given:\*\* A vector $\\vec{A}$ with magnitude $15$ at an angle $\\theta = 60^\\circ$ to the horizontal.



\### Calculations:

\* \*\*Horizontal Component ($A\_x$):\*\*

&#x20;   $$A\_x = 15 \\cdot \\cos(60^\\circ) = 15 \\cdot 0.5 = \\mathbf{7.5}$$

\* \*\*Vertical Component ($A\_y$):\*\*

&#x20;   $$A\_y = 15 \\cdot \\sin(60^\\circ) = 15 \\cdot \\frac{\\sqrt{3}}{2} \\approx \\mathbf{12.99}$$

\## 6. Function Analysis

\*\*Function:\*\* $f(x) = 3x^2 - 12x + 7$



\### Finding Extrema:

1\. \*\*First Derivative:\*\*

&#x20;  $$f'(x) = 6x - 12$$

2\. \*\*Critical Point:\*\*

&#x20;  Set $f'(x) = 0 \\implies 6x = 12 \\implies \\mathbf{x = 2}$

3\. \*\*Second Derivative Test:\*\*

&#x20;  $$f''(x) = 6$$

&#x20;  Since $f''(x) > 0$, the function is concave up, indicating a \*\*Local Minimum\*\*.

4\. \*\*Minimum Value:\*\*

&#x20;  $$f(2) = 3(2)^2 - 12(2) + 7 = 12 - 24 + 7 = \\mathbf{-5}$$



\*\*Result:\*\* Local minimum at \*\*$(2, -5)$\*\*.

\## 7. Logic \& Series (The Fly and Bicycle)

\*\*Scenario:\*\* A bicycle moves toward a wall at $1 \\text{ m/s}$ from $10 \\text{ m}$ away. A fly moves between the bicycle and the wall at $2 \\text{ m/s}$.



\### Solution:

Instead of calculating an infinite series of trips, we calculate the total time the fly is in the air.

1\. \*\*Time until impact:\*\*

&#x20;  $$\\text{time} = \\frac{\\text{distance}}{\\text{speed of bicycle}} = \\frac{10 \\text{ m}}{1 \\text{ m/s}} = 10 \\text{ s}$$

2\. \*\*Total distance for the fly:\*\*

&#x20;  $$\\text{distance} = \\text{speed of fly} \\times \\text{time} = 2 \\text{ m/s} \\times 10 \\text{ s} = \\mathbf{20 \\text{ m}}$$
