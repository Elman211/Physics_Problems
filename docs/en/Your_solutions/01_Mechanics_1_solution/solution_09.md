\# 9. Momentum Comparison



\## Problem Statement

Determine which object has a greater momentum:

1\.  A \*\*2-gram fly\*\* flying at $10 \\text{ m/s}$.

2\.  A \*\*60-gram tennis ball\*\* moving at $1 \\text{ m/s}$.



\---



\## 1. Definition of Momentum

Linear momentum ($p$) is defined as the product of an object's mass ($m$) and its velocity ($v$):

$$p = m \\cdot v$$



To compare the two objects accurately, we must ensure the units are consistent. We will convert mass from grams (g) to \*\*kilograms (kg)\*\*.



\---



\## 2. Calculations



\### Case A: The Fly

\* \*\*Mass ($m\_1$):\*\* $2 \\text{ g} = 0.002 \\text{ kg}$

\* \*\*Velocity ($v\_1$):\*\* $10 \\text{ m/s}$



$$p\_{fly} = 0.002 \\text{ kg} \\cdot 10 \\text{ m/s}$$

$$p\_{fly} = \\mathbf{0.02 \\text{ kg}\\cdot\\text{m/s}}$$



\### Case B: The Tennis Ball

\* \*\*Mass ($m\_2$):\*\* $60 \\text{ g} = 0.060 \\text{ kg}$

\* \*\*Velocity ($v\_2$):\*\* $1 \\text{ m/s}$



$$p\_{ball} = 0.060 \\text{ kg} \\cdot 1 \\text{ m/s}$$

$$p\_{ball} = \\mathbf{0.06 \\text{ kg}\\cdot\\text{m/s}}$$



\---



\## 3. Comparison and Conclusion

Comparing the two results:

\* $p\_{fly} = 0.02 \\text{ kg}\\cdot\\text{m/s}$

\* $p\_{ball} = 0.06 \\text{ kg}\\cdot\\text{m/s}$



Since $0.06 > 0.02$, the \*\*60-gram tennis ball\*\* has a greater momentum than the 2-gram fly.



\---



\## Summary Table

| Object | Mass (kg) | Velocity (m/s) | Momentum (kg·m/s) |

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| \*\*Fly\*\* | $0.002$ | $10$ | $0.02$ |

| \*\*Tennis Ball\*\* | $0.060$ | $1$ | $\\mathbf{0.06}$ |



\*\*Result:\*\* The tennis ball has \*\*3 times\*\* the momentum of the fly.
