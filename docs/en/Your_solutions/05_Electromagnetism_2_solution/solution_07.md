\## Task 7: Wavelength and Frequency of Visible Light



\### Problem Statement

The human eye is most sensitive to light with a wavelength ($\\lambda$) of approximately \*\*550 nm\*\*.

1\. Determine the corresponding color in the visible spectrum.

2\. Calculate the frequency ($f$) of this light.



\### 1. Color Identification

The visible spectrum ranges from approximately $400\\text{ nm}$ (violet) to $700\\text{ nm}$ (red). A wavelength of $550\\text{ nm}$ falls within the center of the visible range.



\* \*\*Result:\*\* This wavelength corresponds to \*\*Green\*\* (specifically, a yellowish-green).







\[Image of visible light spectrum wavelength chart]





\### 2. Frequency Calculation

To find the frequency, we use the fundamental wave speed equation:

$$c = \\lambda f \\implies f = \\frac{c}{\\lambda}$$



\*\*Constants and Conversions:\*\*

\* \*\*Speed of light ($c$):\*\* $\\approx 3 \\times 10^8 \\text{ m/s}$

\* \*\*Wavelength ($\\lambda$):\*\* $550 \\text{ nm} = 550 \\times 10^{-9} \\text{ m} = 5.5 \\times 10^{-7} \\text{ m}$



\*\*Calculation:\*\*

$$f = \\frac{3 \\times 10^8 \\text{ m/s}}{5.5 \\times 10^{-7} \\text{ m}}$$

$$f \\approx 5.45 \\times 10^{14} \\text{ Hz}$$



\*\*Final Result:\*\* The frequency is approximately \*\*$545 \\text{ THz}$\*\* (Terahertz).

\## Task 8: Ordering the EM Spectrum



\### Problem Statement

List the following types of electromagnetic radiation in order of \*\*increasing wavelength\*\*:

\*Infrared, Ultraviolet, Microwaves, X-rays, Radio waves, Gamma rays.\*



\### 1. The Ordered List

In the EM spectrum, wavelength is inversely proportional to frequency and energy. Moving from shortest to longest wavelength:



1\. \*\*Gamma rays\*\* (Shortest wavelength / Highest energy)

2\. \*\*X-rays\*\*

3\. \*\*Ultraviolet\*\*

4\. \*\*Infrared\*\*

5\. \*\*Microwaves\*\*

6\. \*\*Radio waves\*\* (Longest wavelength / Lowest energy)







\### 2. Key Physical Concepts

When explaining this order, keep these three rules in mind:



| Radiation Type | Energy Level | Frequency | Wavelength |

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| \*\*Gamma/X-ray\*\* | Extremely High | Extremely High | Extremely Short |

| \*\*Visible/UV\*\* | Moderate | Moderate | Moderate |

| \*\*Radio/Micro\*\* | Low | Low | Long |



\*\*Rule of Thumb:\*\* Short wavelengths (like Gamma) have enough energy to pass through or damage cells (ionizing radiation), while long wavelengths (like Radio) are used for communication because they can travel long distances and pass around obstacles.
